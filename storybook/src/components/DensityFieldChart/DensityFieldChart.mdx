# DensityFieldChart

Iso-surfaces through a 3D density grid, with an optional void overlay.

`Source: code`

## Anatomy

```
Plotly3DChart
├── volume trace        x/y/z flattened from the cube, value = overdensity
│                       isomin at the 60th percentile, isomax at the 99.5th
│                       surface.count = the iso-surfaces setting, Inferno colorscale
└── scatter3d trace     the void overlay, when voids are supplied
```

A configuration of `Plotly3DChart` with no styles of its own. It turns a `(grid, grid, grid)` overdensity
cube into nested translucent surfaces.

The void overlay draws the VIDE catalog's spheres inside the same box, so underdense regions can be checked
against the field they came from.

## Props

`density` is the cube, `boxSize` its side in Mpc/h, `isoSurfaces` how many nested shells to draw, and
`opacity` how transparent each one is. Both of the last two are exposed as sidebar sliders, because the
readable setting depends entirely on the field.

## Tokens

None of its own. See `Plotly3DChart`.

## Behaviour

- Grid resolution is chosen in the sidebar and changes the request, not just the render.
- More iso-surfaces at high opacity becomes opaque quickly. The defaults, 12 surfaces at 0.08, are chosen to
  stay readable.

## Accessibility

Inherits `Plotly3DChart` entirely, including having no keyboard or screen-reader path.

## Content

The colorbar title is the caller's, and axis titles come from `Plotly3DChart`'s spatial defaults, in Mpc/h.
Percentile bounds are not shown to the reader: the colorbar reports values, not percentiles.


## Use it

- The 3D Density Field statistic.

## Don't use it

- For a 2D slice. Use **Charts / FieldMapMosaic** or the 2D field map.
