# Decision log

Append-only. One entry per decision a future reader could plausibly try to reverse. Component pages link
here; they never restate an entry.

Write an entry when a choice rules something out, contradicts a spec, or rejects the obvious answer for a
reason that is not visible in the result. Everything else is just the component.

Entries below were migrated out of inline code comments, which is where this reasoning used to live.

---

## DR-001 · Invert the Add Plot button

**Status** accepted · **Affects** `TopNav`, `add-plot/surface`, `add-plot/text`

**Context.** Every control in the app sits on a dark surface. The single most important action needs to be
findable without enlarging it.

**Decision.** The nav Add Plot button uses a solid white surface with dark text, the only inverted control in
the interface. It gets its own token pair rather than reusing text and surface tokens, because those are
dark-surface-with-light-text and this is the opposite pairing.

**Rejected.** The accent gradient, which is used by the modal's confirm button. Two gradient buttons on
screen at once made neither read as primary.

---

## DR-002 · Use a dedicated blue for the focused tile ring

**Status** accepted · **Affects** `PlotTile`, `border/selected`

**Context.** Toolbar actions operate on the focused tile, so focus must be visible before those actions are
used.

**Decision.** A 1px ring in `border/selected` (`#3B82F6`), replacing the tile's own border rather than adding
to it, so selection reads as a colour change and not an added thickness.

**Rejected.** A translucent white ring: its contrast depended on whatever chart sat behind it, which is
unreliable by construction. A brand-purple ring: competed with the accent gradient.

---

## DR-003 · Make the note card opaque

**Status** accepted · **Affects** `NoteOverlay`, `note/surface`, `note/border`, `note/text`

**Context.** Notes are placed over charts and are expected to survive being screenshotted into a slide.

**Decision.** A flat, opaque card in `brand-deep-crimson` with white text, verified at 9.3:1 contrast.

**Rejected.** A translucent warm-amber card. A translucent fill's real contrast depends on what sits behind
it, so it could not be verified at all.

---

## DR-004 · Plot tiles and note cards are square-cornered

**Status** accepted · **Affects** `PlotTile`, `NoteOverlay`, `AnnotationOverlay`

**Context.** Rounded corners and shadows both signal "floating object". Applied to everything, they flatten
the hierarchy.

**Decision.** A populated plot tile has `border-radius: 0`, overriding `Tile`'s 12px. Note cards and
annotation callouts are square and shadowless: they sit on the page rather than above it.

**Rejected.** A single radius across every surface, which left nothing able to signal elevation.

---

## DR-005 · Render the select menu in a portal

**Status** accepted · **Affects** `SelectField`, `MultiSelect`, `ChartModeDropdown`

**Context.** Select fields appear inside the add-plot modal's scrollable fields area and inside the params
sidebar, both of which clip overflow.

**Decision.** The menu renders in a `document.body` portal at `position: fixed`, positioned from the
trigger's bounding rect. It closes on scroll rather than repositioning, so it cannot visually detach from a
trigger that moved.

**Rejected.** Absolute positioning inside the field wrapper, which a scrollable ancestor clipped.

---

## DR-006 · Force always-visible scrollbars in long menus

**Status** accepted · **Affects** `SelectField`, `ParamsSidebar`

**Context.** Field pickers can hold well over a hundred options. On macOS, overlay scrollbars are invisible
until the user is already scrolling, so a long list looks complete.

**Decision.** Style `::-webkit-scrollbar` alone, which forces the space-reserving always-visible thumb, and
pair it with a bottom fade that renders only while the list is scrolled short of its end.

**Rejected.** The standard `scrollbar-width` and `scrollbar-color` properties. Setting them alongside
`::-webkit-scrollbar` makes Chromium fall back to the overlay scrollbar, which is the behaviour being fixed.

---

## DR-007 · The canvas is its own surface

**Status** accepted · **Affects** `Viewer`, `surface/canvas`

**Context.** The area behind tiles originally used the same token as the tiles themselves, so a tile had no
edge against its background.

**Decision.** `surface/canvas` (`--neutral-960`) is darker than `surface/content` and distinct from
`surface/chrome`. Three surfaces, three roles.

---

## DR-008 · Chrome is 48px, not 64px

**Status** accepted · **Affects** `TopNav`, `IconRail`, `LoadingIndicator`

**Context.** The shell consumed more vertical and horizontal space than the plots it framed.

**Decision.** Top nav height and icon rail width are both 48px. Children scaled proportionally: rail items
and the Add Plot button are 32px, rail icons 16px, nav button text 13px.

---

## DR-009 · Fix the add-plot modal's field area height

**Status** accepted · **Affects** `AddPlotModal`

**Context.** The Curated and Custom tabs have very different content heights, so the dialog resized when
switching tabs.

**Decision.** The fields area is a fixed 260px of content height, measured from the Curated tab's own settled
height. Custom scrolls within it.

**Rejected.** Letting the dialog hug its content, which moved the tab row and the footer under the cursor.

---

## DR-010 · Replace the min/max slider pair with a range filter

**Status** accepted · **Affects** `RangeFilterControl`, Custom plot pattern

**Context.** Each catalog filter was two full-width sliders, so three filters filled the modal.

**Decision.** One `RangeFilterControl` per field: typeable min and max boxes above a dual-thumb track,
matching the shape FlatHUB itself uses.

---

## DR-011 · Withdraw click-to-pin in 3D scenes

**Status** accepted · **Affects** `Plotly3DChart`, `DensityFieldChart`, `ParticleCloudChart`

**Context.** Clicking a 3D scene pinned a marker and read out its coordinates. In a volume render the click
also has a scalar value worth reading; in a particle cloud it does not, so the pin showed coordinates alone.
Pinning also competed with orbiting for the same click.

**Decision.** `pinEnabled` defaults to `false` and every call site passes `false` explicitly, so the feature
cannot return by omission. Measurement in a 3D scene is the Ruler tool's job.

**Rejected.** Keeping it behind a per-caller toggle. The click conflict is in the interaction model, not in
the callers, so a toggle would have left the same problem switchable.
